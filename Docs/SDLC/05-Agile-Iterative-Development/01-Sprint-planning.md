# Sprint Planning

Choose one concrete outcome from the [product backlog](https://github.com/Farhad-V/Web-Resumefolio/issues). State the Sprint Goal in terms of what can be demonstrated. Select the work needed to meet it, check dependencies and acceptance criteria, and identify what to verify.

Record the goal, selected issue links, acceptance checks and any unresolved prerequisite in the work item or board. Refine unclear items in [Backlog Refinement](02-Backlog-refinement.md) and carry the selected work into [Detailed Analysis & Design](03-Detailed-analysis-and-design.md).

The first candidate goal is a repeatable local setup for Resumefolio and ProjectForge. See [Project Setup](00-Project-setup.md) for its acceptance checks. No sprint execution is recorded here.
