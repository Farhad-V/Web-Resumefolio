# Requirements Answer Review

Current answers and their MVP/future dispositions are recorded once in [Policy Decisions](../../../baseline/policy-decisions.md). The [question index](open-questions-and-decisions.md) preserves Q-D01–28 navigation and the owner-accepted profile policy.

Review an affected answer against its source requirement, applicable [criterion disposition](../../../baseline/criterion-dispositions.csv), actor/denial/failure/recovery scenarios and the relevant Design. Record actual findings and evidence; selected specification does not mean the running capability has passed.

Use the [existing review cases](../04-scenarios-and-models/user-stories-and-scenarios.md), [review findings](../06-validation-and-traceability/review-plan-and-findings.md), [baseline review criteria](requirements-review-and-baseline-criteria.md) and [implementation gates](../../../baseline/implementation-sequence.md). Future provider/runner topics retain their deferred scope. No new owner acceptance or test result is inferred from this navigation page.
